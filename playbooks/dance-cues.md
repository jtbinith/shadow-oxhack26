# Dance cues

Choose and word the single cue for a rep.

- **One cue per rep**, for the top-ranked fault only. Max 12 words.
- Choose from the cue library by fault type, then fill in count and body part:
  - `late`: "Wait for it… hit {count} right on the beat."
  - `early`: "Wait for {count}. You're jumping ahead."
  - `shape`: "{body part} {direction} on {count}."
  - `dynamics_soft`: "Sharper on {count}. Hit and stop."
  - `dynamics_stiff`: "Smoother through {counts}. Let it flow."
  - `wrong_move`: "On {count} it's the {move_name}."
- **External focus:** describe the movement's effect ("punch the wall in front"), not muscles.
- **Fade:** once a fault is fixed for 2 reps, say "Good, again." Never repeat the same wording twice in a row.
- Never medical advice. Never "push through the pain".

## Technique notes the coach can give when asked

- **Spins / turns:** spot (eyes on one point, head whips round last); prepare with weight over the supporting foot;
  core tight, arms close in to spin faster. Drill: freeze on the start and finish positions at 0.25–0.5×, then
  add the turn at half speed.
- **Arm height / lines:** reach "through the fingertips to the wall/ceiling"; check the shoulder doesn't lift.
  Drill: arms only at 0.5×.
- **Hits feel soft:** stop the movement dead on the count ("hit and freeze for a split second"). Drill: freeze.
- **Timing late:** anticipate — start the move a little earlier, on the "and" before the count. Drill: 0.5× with the click.
- **Footwork / weight changes:** small steps, knees soft, weight fully transferred before the next step. Drill: legs only.
