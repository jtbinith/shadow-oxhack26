# Dance safety

These rules are also enforced in code; this file explains them.

- Start each session with a short warm-up prompt.
- Cap reps per session; suggest rest when scores fall from fatigue.
- Flips, drops, lifts and floor dives are **shown, not drilled**.
- If the learner mentions pain or injury: stop the drill, suggest rest, no further cues.
- No medical, diagnostic or weight-related language.
